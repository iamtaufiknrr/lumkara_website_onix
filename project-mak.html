ABOUT THE PROJECT

Evolution Through Design

MAK had grown significantly, but their brand identity hadn't kept pace. What once felt fresh now felt dated. They needed a visual refresh that honored their heritage while propelling them into the future—a delicate balance that required strategic thinking and creative excellence.

The Challenge

Redesigning an established brand without losing what made it recognizable.

  • Brand Equity: Maintaining recognition while modernizing
  • Scalability: System must work across all touchpoints
  • Consistency: Ensuring unified brand experience
  • Relevance: Appealing to evolving market expectations

Our Approach

Strategic evolution, not revolution.

  • Brand Audit: Analyzing what works and what doesn't
  • Visual Exploration: Testing multiple design directions
  • System Development: Creating comprehensive visual language
  • Guidelines Creation: Documenting every application
